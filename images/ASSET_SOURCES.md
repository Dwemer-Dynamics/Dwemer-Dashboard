Provenance for the homepage card-effect art and header logo. These images are
third-party material: they are not covered by this project's GPL licence and
keep their owners' terms (see [LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md)). No licence is
claimed or granted here.

## Card effects (`card-effects/`)

Cropped, resized and re-encoded from the games or from UESP-hosted images.
Used only as decorative layers or CSS masks on the official mod cards.
